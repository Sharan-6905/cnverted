# Lead-flow logo assets

- X, Reddit, Product Hunt, GitHub, G2, Crunchbase, and YouTube: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0. Downloaded from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/{slug}.svg`; brand fill colors added to the root SVG.
- Claude, OpenAI (used for ChatGPT), Kimi, and Gemini: [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), MIT. Downloaded from `https://unpkg.com/@lobehub/icons-static-svg@latest/icons/{slug}.svg` on 2026-09-26. Full license is retained in `LOBE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- LinkedIn's supplied Figma vector is also stored as `linkedin.svg`, with the faded opacity removed and its blue tile restored to `#0A66C2`; see [LinkedIn brand guidance](https://brand.linkedin.com/in-logo). The original Figma asset is preserved.
- Workflow logos retain the full-color Figma artwork in `public/figma/`.
- Composio: original 180 × 180 brand icon from its [official website](https://composio.dev/apple-touch-icon.png), downloaded September 29, 2026 and stored unchanged as `composio.png` for the integrations strip.
- Kimi's white/blue asset uses a dark tile for legibility. OpenAI's Blossom remains monochrome, following its [brand guidance](https://openai.com/brand/); glass effects apply to the surrounding tile, not the logo itself.

All SVGs are served locally. Brand marks identify the platforms and models described by the site owner; they do not indicate endorsement.
