Our story

From idea to execution.

CNVRTED exists for ambitious founders and sales teams who want to move from "thinking about it" to actually closing deals. Whether you're a solo founder running your first outbound, or a sales team scaling multi-touch campaigns — CNVRTED gives you verified data and the tools to convert it.

The old way

Most platforms focus on polished companies that already have a process. They hand you a spreadsheet and call it a pipeline.

The CNVRTED way

We care about the messy, real stage — the late nights, the first users, the failed experiments. We built tools that work for that.

CNVRTED is designed for people who are actively building — not just consuming content about startups.
