# GTM motion film

An 81-second coded loop for “Squeeze market in minutes.” The fictional seller Halcyon provides customer-support automation to growing DTC home brands. The same fictional account, Bloomcart, and buyer, Maya Chen, carry the story from discovery to CRM.

## Storyboard

| Time | Composition and action |
|---|---|
| 0–7s | Halcyon's brief resolves into an ICP: DTC home brands, 50–200 people, Head of CX. |
| 7–15s | The ICP expands into pains, tools, decision-makers, and qualification rules. |
| 15–23s | A public reference profile grounds the segment; a fictional account table is built and poor-fit accounts are deprioritised. |
| 23–32s | LinkedIn, Reddit and website/job-page examples connect to Bloomcart. Signals are labelled examples, not actual scraped posts. |
| 32–40s | Three matching accounts become an enriched contact table; Maya's profile resolves with source context and a reserved .example email. |
| 40–49s | The buyer's trigger becomes a personalised email; approval precedes simulated sending. |
| 49–57s | A follow-up receives a reply; the remaining follow-up is cancelled. |
| 57–64s | The reply becomes a calendar selection and a confirmed meeting. |
| 64–72s | The same opportunity moves from discovery through an accepted proposal to closed won. |
| 72–81s | The record syncs to HubSpot; the win sharpens the ICP, then the composition loops. |

## Art direction

Existing site tokens: violet #4f65f5, lavender #ac89e8, green #177c5a, charcoal #242a29, warm cream #fffaf2. Reuse paper texture, editorial headings and local original fictional portrait assets. Platform logos retain their original colors. A scoped GSAP timeline animates transforms/opacity and connected source paths; no scrolling, loaders or progress HUD. Offscreen/hidden-tab pause, reduced-motion overview, keyboard pause.
